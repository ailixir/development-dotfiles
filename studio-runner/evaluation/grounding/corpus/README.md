Screening evidence, not ADR-SC-005 D-C evidence. `tasks.draft.jsonl` is the draft the orchestrator freezes into
`tasks.jsonl` and `freeze.json`. Nothing here was written from, or checked against, the product collector's output.
Pages were read with Playwright's public `ariaSnapshot`, `getByRole` counts and screenshots only.

## Task file extensions (beyond INTERFACES.md)

- `core` (bool): member of the recommended balanced subset.
- `image` (random stratum): `random/rnd-NNN.png`.
- `negCheck` (negatives): `{kind:'absent', chain}` (chain matches 0) or `{kind:'ambiguous', min, chain}` (chain matches at least `min`). `verify.mjs` checks it, so every "absent target" is really absent and every planted ambiguity is real. Negatives and class-N fixtures have `gt: null` at task and step level.
- Chain args: strings, numbers, booleans, objects. A regular expression is `{ "regex": "...", "flags": "i" }`. Inside `filter`, `has`/`hasNot` take `{ "chain": [...] }` (resolved from `page`, as Playwright does).
- Operations: `fill` and `selectOption` carry `value` (the quoted literal in the text); `press` carries `key`; `assert` carries `predicate` and, for `containsText`/`hasValue`, `expected`; a context-reuse `fill` carries `valueFromStep: <index>` (the value `read` captured in that earlier step, `innerText` trimmed).
- `setup` ops are `{op:'click', gt}` only (disclosures opened before the step).
- `expect` on negatives is the preferred refusal. Per the plan, any non-ACTION answer counts as handled, and a false ACTION or COMPLETE is confident-wrong. Three negatives are `UNSUPPORTED` by nature (load time, CPU, second tab). `fx-e3` (iframe) is also `UNSUPPORTED`.

## Step text and ground truth

Step text describes what a person sees (visible text, labels, placement, section) in the existing style. Where a visible
word differs from the accessible name, the visible word is in the text (`Click the "Menu" button`, GT `Show navigation menu`).
`menuNameDiffers` has 14 tasks: gov.uk (6), wikipedia (2: "hide"), openlibrary (2), rfc-editor (2: "Show more"), usa.gov ("Top"),
MDN ("Theme"). The python.org links whose name comes from a CSS `>>>` prefix were moved out of that stratum, because the
user sees the arrows. The MDN "Log in" link is an icon with no visible text, so it is an `iconOnly` task described as an icon.

GT chains prefer `getByRole` with exact names, scoped by landmark (`banner`, `main`, `contentinfo`, named `navigation`),
by group, or by a row/section `has` filter. Exceptions, all intentional:
- 52 tasks use `nth`/`first`/`last` because the step wording is ordinal ("the second story", "the dropdown above the results").
- 64 tasks use a `locator(css)` hop for unnamed controls (`input[name="q"]`, `#term`, `.age a`) or old layouts.
- Visual-only wiki-vis-01: the field has only a `title`, which Playwright's role engine does not use for the name.

## Fixture regression

`fixture://<case>/<page>` maps to `<origin>/ns/<case>[.<tag>]/<page>` on `evaluation/observed-authoring/pages/server.mjs`
(`startCorpusPages()`; `page` is the html file name without `.html`). `verify.mjs` does this with a per-load tag such as `v1g3`.
One task per case with an element-targeting first step: c01, c03, c04, c05, c06, c07, c13, e2, e4, q1, q2, q3 (ACTION) and the class-N
cases c11 (UNSUPPORTED), c12 (CLARIFICATION, four unlabeled buttons), e3 (UNSUPPORTED, field inside an iframe).
Not included: c02 (URL assertion), c08 and c09 (assertions whose truth is decided at run time), c10, d1, d3, inc-edit4,
inc-insert (no page in the directory, or no element-targeting step). `fx-e4` is adapted: the original clicks Item 0420 after
scrolling the virtual list, and the task schema has no scroll setup op, so it clicks Item 0003.

## Random stratum

- Seed **20261026**, `mulberry32`. Sampling: pick a pool page uniformly, then one of its interactive aria refs uniformly
  (`button, link, textbox, searchbox, combobox, checkbox, radio, switch, tab, menuitem, spinbutton` from
  `page.ariaSnapshot({mode:'ai'})`). This weights pages equally rather than refs, so Wikipedia's 1,558 refs do not dominate.
- Pool: the 15 distinct no-setup real-site URLs of the corpus, minus six volatile-feed pages (HN front page and `/newest`,
  Wikipedia Main_Page, Steam search, Open Library search, the weather forecast page), whose GT would void within hours.
  The exclusion is by page, decided before drawing, never by element.
- 41 draws, 40 accepted, 1 rejected as a duplicate draw. Other rejection rules (target under 8 px, `normalize()` not unique
  or resolving to another element, parse failure) never fired. Roles: 33 links, 2 buttons, 1 checkbox, 3 textboxes, 1 searchbox.
  17 of 40 are on held-out sites.
- GT: `locator('aria-ref=eN').normalize()` converted to `gt.chain` by `locatorStringToChain` (a small parser in `probe.mjs`),
  then re-resolved: count 1 and the same element as the aria ref.
- Crop: `page.screenshot({clip, fullPage:true})` around the element box plus a 220x130 px margin; the outline (white halo, red line)
  is drawn on a canvas in a separate `about:blank` page. The target page's DOM is never touched.
- `steps[0].text` is empty. Operation by role and tag: click for links/buttons/tabs/switches, `check` for checkbox/radio,
  `fill` with the literal `spk07` for text inputs and input-based comboboxes (a `<select>` combobox would be `selectOption`; none was drawn).
  The brief said "selectOption for combobox"; an `<input role=combobox>` cannot take `selectOption`, so I keyed on the tag.
- Blind writers must put the literal `spk07` in quotes in the text of `fill` tasks.

## Verification

`verify.mjs`, 463 tasks, 2 fresh contexts each at 1280x720, 2.5 s settle after `domcontentloaded`, Playwright 1.63.0, run on 2026-10-26.
Single-step tasks with the same URL and setup share one page load per context and are never performed. Chain tasks get their
own load per context; each step is resolved, then performed by its GT locator before the next one is resolved (the last step is
only resolved). Setup ops must also resolve to exactly one element.

Result of the last full run: **463 of 463 pass** (every GT chain matched exactly one element in both contexts; every `negCheck`
held; every chain step resolved after the previous step ran). By stratum all 13 are 100%.
The last two consecutive full runs (concurrency 3) were both 463 of 463. `verify-report.json` has the date, counts, visibility and, for any chain that matched other than once, the page URL, title and text length per context. Failures found and fixed while authoring:
`wiki-vis-01` (CSS matched 3 elements), `pyorg-dup-07` (heading name not exact because of a CSS icon), `usa-chain-02`
(the verifier waited on a slow post-click navigation; the verifier now never performs a chain's last step).

I also printed the tag, text, aria-label and neighbouring label of the GT element for all 365 single-step real-site answerable tasks
and read them against the step text, then looked at screenshots for arXiv, Open Library, MDN and RFC Editor. That found four wording
problems, now fixed: MDN "Log in" (hidden name), RFC Editor "From year"/"To year" (hidden labels), the python.org `>>>` links, and a
named "Go" button filed as visual-only. This is a human spot-check, not a proof; uniqueness is what `verify.mjs` proves.

## Known limitations

- **Volatile content.** `hn-*` ordinal tasks, `hn-read-*`, `wx-read-*`, `wx-field-*` headline, `pyorg-read-*`, `pyorg-dup-07` (version), `ol-nav-06`
  ("155 editions"), `steam-*` results and prices, `rfc-sel-01` (option counts) and `govuk-read-04` depend on live data. Their GT is
  unique today and may VOID on another day; that is the VOID rule working, but expect some next-day attrition. `arxiv-icon-01`
  needs the announcement banner, which arXiv removes after the maintenance notice.
- **Cookie banners.** Only GOV.UK has a real cookie banner. Wikipedia's banner in the probe is a fundraiser. Other sites showed none from this location
  and may show consent dialogs in the EU or UK.
- **Geography.** weather.gov and Steam prices depend on region; craigslist was dropped for that reason.
- **Transient flake on gutenberg.org.** In two earlier runs a gutenberg task failed in one of its two contexts because the page had no "Search books" box (count 0: `gut-chain-02`, `gut-field-02`). I could not reproduce it in 8 isolated loads or in 6 repeated runs of the gutenberg tasks, so I treat it as a server-side variant seen during bursts of repeated loads. The VOID rule covers it. Re-run `verify.mjs --only gut-` if a freeze check trips on it.
- **Rate limits.** Open Library is slow and reset one connection while I was authoring; lobste.rs rate-limited me. Keep per-host concurrency low
  (verify with `--concurrency 2` for Open Library if it flakes). Verification was run at concurrency 3-4.
- **Perception vs. GT.** GT uniqueness is proved; that the step text points at the intended element is only spot-checked by me.
  The stratum labels are my judgement (for example `wiki-vis-01` is "unnamed" for Playwright, while Chromium's accessibility tree
  may name it from the `title`).
- **Not covered.** No dialog/modal tasks, no iframe or shadow-DOM tasks on real sites (shadow DOM only in fixture e2), no file uploads, no
  login-gated pages, no drag/hover. Hovering menus (python.org "Downloads") are not exercised.
- **The random stratum's pool is 15 pages**, link-heavy (33 of 40), and excludes feed pages, so it is a uniform sample of that pool, not of the web.
