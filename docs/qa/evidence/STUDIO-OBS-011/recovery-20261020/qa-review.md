Technical limits: process inspection and `kill` are separate standard OS operations, so the identity/tree checks are snapshots, not an atomic PID handle or a guarantee against every concurrent change. The signal polling allowances total at most ten seconds of configured waits; `ps`/`lsof` execution and scheduling add overhead and have no subprocess timeout. Those limits do not invalidate this narrow, identified idle-tree recovery and must not be described as an atomic proof or a hard total wall-clock deadline. If observation changes or the script exits with an error, recovery completion remains unproven; this review provides no permission to widen targets or improvise further termination.
