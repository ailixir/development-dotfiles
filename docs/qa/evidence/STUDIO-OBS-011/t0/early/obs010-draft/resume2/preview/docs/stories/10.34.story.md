## User Story

**As** the Epic 10 owner (@pm), on behalf of the epic's Definition of done,
**I want** a single, named story that executes every externally-blocked Epic 10 acceptance criterion once its gate opens, and records each result in the staging-acceptance register,
**so that** the epic is not held open forever on a technicality — a Blocked criterion has a named home that will one day tick it, rather than a merged story frozen in `InReview` with no mechanism to ever reach `Done`.

This is the mechanism 10.19 itself names for its own closure: *"The story is Done only when every criterion is either executed and passed, or superseded by a named follow-up story (10.34+, @pm's call) that carries it."* (`10.19.story.md:247`). Ten other merged stories name the same mechanism, in the same words, for the same reason (`10.19.story.md:247` is quoted verbatim in the Status row of 10.5, 10.6, 10.7, 10.8, 10.9, 10.11, 10.12, 10.13, 10.15, 10.16 and 10.18 — each corrected by @po on 2026-09-17 as closure-plan step 0.4).

## Problem Statement

### What this story is, and what it is not

**This story is an execution vehicle, not a construction project.** It does not build staging, does not implement the trusted identity gateway, does not stand up the internal Playwright image, does not write the egress policy mechanism, and does not rule on any open architecture question. Every one of those is another team's, another agent's, or another story's — named individually below.

**It is not a re-specification.** Every acceptance criterion in this story is quoted verbatim from the story that drafted it, with that story's own id, its own blocking gate, and — where one exists — its own falsification/evidence requirement. Where a criterion's original wording contains an error, this story names the error and defers the fix to whoever owns that field (usually @po); it does not silently correct another story's file.

**It is not a dumping ground.** Every criterion below traces to a named origin story, a named id, a named blocking gate (`ED-0n` and/or `SPK-0n`, or — for exactly one criterion, marked apart — an unruled architecture amendment), and a named recording location in `docs/qa/staging-acceptance-register.md`. A criterion whose origin could not be established does not appear here; it appears in the drafting report as a question instead (see *Verification of the inherited inventory*, below).

**It cannot start.** As of this draft, all twelve external items are open, the fold has not begun, and one criterion (AC-24) is blocked on an architecture ruling that has no ETA in any source and is independent of the fold and the twelve gates entirely. See *Why this story cannot start*, at the end.

### Why this story exists

`EPIC-10-CLOSURE-PLAN.md` (independent review, 2026-09-17) found that the epic is not administratively closed by its own records, in part because *"the epic's own Definition of done… gives the mechanism: superseded by a named follow-up story (10.34+, @pm's call) that carries it. Without that story, DoD item 1 is unsatisfiable and the epic stays open forever on a technicality"* (§3(a), quoting 10.19's own Definition of done). Step 0.6 of that plan's closure sequence is: *"Decision: draft story 10.34 — Staging acceptance execution carrying, by name, 10.19 AC-1…AC-7, 10.16 AC-19, and the fifteen 'deferred halves' in register §3."* The user was offered a narrower variant and a variant excluding the architecture-blocked item (AC-24, below) and chose the full inherited set. This story is that decision, drafted.

## Definition of done *(RULED by @po at validation, 2026-09-17)*

The draft offered two shapes and declined to choose. **The ruling is SCOPED, with one condition that closes the loophole scoping would otherwise open.**

> **This story is Done when every criterion except AC-24 is executed and passed, and AC-24 has been transferred to a named carrier. It is not Done while AC-24 sits here with no carrier named.**

**Why scoped and not strict.** Three reasons, in order of weight.

1. **The governing sentence already allows it.** Epic 10's Definition of done, which this whole mechanism descends from, reads: *"The story is Done only when every criterion is either executed and passed, **or superseded by a named follow-up story… that carries it**"* (`10.19.story.md:247`). The disjunct is in the rule itself. A criterion may be discharged by **transfer to a named carrier**, not only by execution — that is precisely the mechanism that created this story. Strict would apply a rule to 10.34 that the rule does not state, for the one criterion it most obviously covers.
2. **Strict reproduces the pathology this story was created to cure.** 10.34's own User Story: *"the epic is not held open forever on a technicality — a Blocked criterion has a named home that will one day tick it, rather than a merged story frozen in `InReview` with no mechanism to ever reach `Done`."* Under strict, 10.34 becomes exactly that frozen story. Solving 10.18's and 10.19's problem by transplanting it into 10.34 is not solving it.
3. **AC-24 is not the same kind of "not yet" as the other thirty-two.** Every other criterion is blocked on something that *will arrive on its own schedule* — an environment, a spike record, a commit. AC-24 is blocked on a decision nobody has been asked to make, with no owner, no ETA and no named implementer. Holding a story's completion on it is holding it on a null.

**The condition, because scoping without one is a loophole.** @sm was right that the two shapes are not symmetrical: scoped, carelessly applied, lets a criterion be excluded from Done and then quietly forgotten — which is the failure mode that hid `ED-02`'s ownership gap for five waves and produced this epic's standing rule against closure by silence. So:

- **AC-24 stays listed here, unticked, with its gate and @po's dissent intact,** until it is transferred. It is not deleted and its checkbox is never ticked by this story.
- **It is discharged only by a transfer that names a carrier** — a story id, not a description. `"whichever story is open when the ruling lands"` is **not a name**, which is this criterion's actual defect and is why 10.11 could not discharge it either.
- **@po closes the "not a name" defect now, so the transfer is executable the day the ruling lands** rather than starting another search: **the carrier of record for AC-24 is whichever story @pm names within the same wave as @architect's ruling; if @pm names none, the default carrier is a successor to 10.11c, drafted by @sm, id assigned by @pm.** *(This is @po's decision, not a source citation. Reason: 10.11c is the story that escalated the Class C amendment and owns the review surface that would render the confirmed revision; a successor on that line is the only placement that needs no new context. Article II is untouched — the **ruling** remains @architect's alone; this names only where the **implementation** lands afterwards.)*
- **@qa may not mark this story Done** while AC-24 has no named carrier. That is the test of the condition, and it is checkable in one read.

**What this ruling does not do.** It does not re-open the user's decision to include AC-24 (settled, and not reopenable), does not ask @architect to rule, does not ask staging to produce evidence for it, and does not change who owns the ruling. @po's dissent at AC-24 stands as recorded.
