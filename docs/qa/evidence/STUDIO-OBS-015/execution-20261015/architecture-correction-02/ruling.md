## Confirmed counterexample

A verified prefix imports read binding B at step 2 and a later `fill(binding B)` consumer at step 3. Its allocation already records the step-3 fill intent. Admission attaches a consumer edge before the native action executes. The owning runner consumes attempt 0, positively refuses before native invocation, and commits the exact authenticated no-invocation receipt and drift switch. First suffix learning replaces the unperformed fill with a literal fill.

The raw allocation consumer and the admitted failed edge remain in the database. V14 `studio_guided_allocation_identity` deliberately forbids shrinking consumer intent; consumer edges are append-only history. The final effective plan has the successfully executed read and no binding consumer. Current Java `graph()` and the pure runner both equate final need consumers with raw allocation consumers and expect every raw edge to name an effective action. That rejects this valid, previously approved path.

The correction is a local effective projection, after validating complete original facts. Do not delete historical rows, shrink allocation intent, hide failed records from the basis, rewrite metadata hashes or relax the model's need rules.

## Exact projection

1. First run the existing complete ledger, scope, historical version, preparation, association, no-invocation receipt, owner/fence and immediate-base validations. A failed/unknown/admitted/dispatching action cannot silently disappear. A receipt hash proves integrity only; production callback authentication and the monotonic native fence remain mandatory. Validate firstSuffixAcceptance's exact session/revision/retained-admission joins and one committed switch.
2. Index effective actions by admission ID and semantic coordinate. They are all SUCCEEDED and every actual successful action appears exactly once. Index raw producers by binding ID. Every raw producer must join one of those effective read admissions with exact coordinate, operation binding ID, recipe, origin and historical availability context/worker/fence. Never filter out a raw producer to hide a successful read or manufacture one for a failed read.
3. Partition every raw consumer edge by its exact consumer admission. An edge either joins an effective successful action or joins the single original excluded consumed attempt. The excluded case must match that full failed attempt, its original proposal/preparation/association, binding argument, argument slot, coordinate, producer admission, destination origin and exact accepted no-native receipt. A PRE_ADMISSION receipt has no admitted action or edge to exclude. Any other raw edge rejects the publication; an absent or generic failure is not exclusion authority.
4. Effective consumer edges are only the successful partition. Require a bijection: every successful binding-valued operation has exactly its matching edge and every effective edge names that operation, valid producer, coordinate, origin and slot. Never collapse two different admitted actions merely because their allocation intent has the same coarse `(stepId,argumentSlot)` pair. Earlier successful ordinals inside drift step j remain present even when a later same-step/slot attempt is excluded.
5. Derive each final executable binding from its effective successful producer and effective edges, using the existing deterministic edge/transfer ordering and unchanged binding semantic projection. Its final need must have the same needId, bindingId and producerStepId as the raw allocation. Final `need.consumers` is exactly the unique coarse pair set exercised by effective edges. Preserve the final plan's own array bytes/order; do not invent a new canonical consumer sort or rewrite its hash. Every final consumer must belong to raw cumulative intent, and every actual successful pair must occur in the final need. This replaces raw-array equality only where the following narrow historical exception applies.
6. A raw allocation consumer absent from the final effective need is allowed only if it is proven original trusted prefix-import intent: the verified base binding and verified PrefixProgram contain the exact binding/consumer action and slot, the consumer is within the originally imported prefix, and its unperformed coordinate is at or after the effective drift boundary. First-suffix acceptance must authorize that replacement. The complete ledger must show no successful use being omitted. Unadmitted future prefix obligations need no fabricated failed action; an admitted action may be omitted only under the sole authenticated excluded-attempt rule. A new model-created consumer, old suffix consumer accidentally imported, missing producer, lost acknowledgement, policy/assertion failure or native-started uncertainty never qualifies.
7. A raw allocation with no successful producer may remain history without an executable need only when its read is a verified original prefix import at or after the drift coordinate, never actually succeeded, and the one first suffix replacement dropped that unperformed meaning. It must have no effective consumer, successful producer, unaccounted raw edge or new model-only intent. A sole positively noninvoked failed read can qualify through the same exact receipt rule. Ordinary/model-created orphan allocations still reject. Do not refund quota, reuse the spent ID for unrelated data or delete the raw allocation to recover capacity.
8. A successful producer with zero final consumers remains an executable binding only under the existing rule: it is the exact trusted prefix read in the immutable retainedPrefixAdmissionIds, with all original recipe/origin/availability joins valid. This includes earlier successful ordinals inside j. Keep its need with consumers `[]` and its read obligation, even if the suffix model does not mention it. A new model need requires one or more consumers; the trusted server merge alone carries the retained zero-consumer producer. A suffix re-learned producer without retained-prefix provenance cannot claim the zero-consumer exception.
9. Do not equate raw allocation count with final needs/producers. The effective binding set is exactly the successful producer set and final executable need set. Extra raw allocations are permitted only under step 7. All raw producer facts remain accounted for. Compute candidate bindings/transfers from this local projection without mutating the basis arrays. For ordinary learning, EMPTY_PREFIX and normal boundary paths with no superseded prefix intent, retain the existing exact checks; this is not a blanket subset rule.
