## Size and split verdict

**Size: L, confirmed against the manifest, not re-derived downward despite the small amount of net-new code.** The size driver is not implementation volume — one component fix (`ChecksLayout`), two small scripts, and several markdown artifacts is not, by itself, an L. It is the **registration surface**: twelve external-dependency/spike ids, each cross-referenced against every one of eighteen prior stories' own deferred halves, seven of which become full Blocked acceptance criteria in their own right (Group A–C), each carrying its own owner, its own gate, and its own "must not silently pass" register entry (AC-15). Undercounting that surface is exactly the failure mode ADR-002 §27's `O-18` gap and the `ED-02` five-wave ownership gap both demonstrate: a real gap that is small in lines of code and large in "nobody is watching this."

**Split verdict: no split.** Applying the epic's own test — *different trees, different reviewers, different gates, and can each half assert its own criteria?* — to the obvious candidate (a "local" story: AC-8–AC-19, versus a "pilot" story: AC-1–AC-7): the trees differ (`frontend/`+`docs/` versus mostly nothing, since AC-1–AC-7 are all `Blocked, skipped, never passed` placeholders with no code to review), but the pilot half **fails the other three prongs outright**. It cannot be independently reviewed (there is no diff to review — every criterion is a recorded blocker, not a change), it has no gate of its own to pass until Platform/SRE and R2D2 close twelve external items, and it therefore cannot assert its own criteria in any sense other than "still blocked," which is exactly what this story's Group A–C already do. No prior Epic-10 split (10.7a/b/c, 10.11a/b/c, 10.16a/b, 10.18a/b) has ever separated a story's "local" half from its "blocked" half for this reason — every one of them carried its own blocked halves alongside its local work, in the same story, because a blocked half is a **registration**, not a **body of work with its own gate**. Splitting 10.19 that way would produce a second story that could never leave `Draft` on its own merits. **If and when Platform/SRE begins closing specific `ED-*`/`SPK-*` items, the epic owner (@pm) may choose to promote the newly-unblocked criterion into its own follow-up story** (decade-block numbering, **10.34+**, since 10.33 was taken by PROD-1 at this story's validation) rather than reopening this one — that is a future decision for whoever is running the epic then, not this draft's to make.
