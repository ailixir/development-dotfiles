## Story

As a Studio author who edits step 4 of a 10-step test, I want the next Run to repeat steps 1–3 from what it already learned and learn only from step 4 onward, so that fixing one step costs seconds of replay plus the learning of the changed tail, never a whole-test re-learn.

## Acceptance Criteria

Binding (ADR-SC-005 D-D, user correction 2026-10-09): "in case we have a 10-step test and we change step 4, only step 4 and beyond (5..10) should be regenerated." The UX statement is **"Learn again" re-learns from the first changed or drifted step, not the whole test.** Rules and defaults below are the ADR's; nothing here reopens the compile-path INC-001 spec.

1. **AC-1 — Server-side change detection k.** Each published learned plan is an immutable per-step snapshot (revision) with per-step hashes over authored text, referenced variable/secret declarations and step-level configuration, keyed by the stable `AuthoringStep` UUIDs INC-001 introduced (reused, not re-invented). On edit, the first changed step **k** is the first index whose hash differs from the published revision; inserts, deletes and reorders count at the first affected index; multiple edits take the earliest; a test-level configuration change (environment, which selects the approved origins; viewport, timeout, browser) ⇒ k = 1, while a governed-policy or `TrustedPolicy` change never changes k; no edit since the last publish ⇒ `READY` (plain replay); first ever Run ⇒ `UNLEARNED` (full learn). `GET /tests/{id}` readiness reports `NEEDS_RELEARN{firstChangedStepId: k}`; `POST /tests/{id}/execute` on a `NEEDS_RELEARN` test ⇒ `409 LEARN_REQUIRED` (OBS-012A vocabulary). Unit tests cover each rule with named fixtures. [ADR-SC-005 D-D; defaults "Readiness vocabulary"; placement map row OBS-015]

2. **AC-2 — Contract change (v3, one generation).** `commands#start` gains `{baseRevisionId, firstLearnStepId}` — **server-computed**; a client `firstLearnStepId` positioned after the server's k in the current draft (it would skip a changed or drifted step) is refused with `409 REVISION_CONFLICT` and `messageCode` `FIRST_LEARN_STEP_NARROWED`, and one at or before k is accepted (the author may choose to re-learn more, never less); position is the step ID's index in the current draft authoring, never the UUID value; session state `REPLAYING_PREFIX` is added; `learned-plan`/`revision` gain per-step snapshot hashes and `reusedFrom{revisionId, stepId}` provenance; result3 reports `relearnedFrom` (the first learned step, and the drift switch point j when applicable); generated bundles regenerated via `tools/build-studio-observed-schemas.cjs`; fixtures extended; `StudioContractTest`/`confinement.test.ts` pins re-anchored with old/new quoted; sequenced through the one contract owner with OBS-013/014. [ADR-SC-005 placement map row OBS-015 "contract change in v3"; "Contracts and pins"]

3. **AC-3 — Mixed-mode session in the guided mode.** A Run on `NEEDS_RELEARN` starts a guided session with `baseRevisionId`; the session enters `REPLAYING_PREFIX` and replays steps 1…k-1 from the published plan through the **observed adapter** on the recorded guided browser (OBS-013 handle), model-free (gateway counters zero; both gateways silent), recapturing fresh binding values under their **existing server-issued binding IDs** and applying the same drift refusals and the OBS-007 `secretRef` rules unchanged; then it switches to learning at k through n. The suffix SOURCE_PLAN sees the full checklist but plans only k…n; bindings produced in 1…k-1 are available to k…n planning under their existing IDs. Steps after k are **always** re-learned even when their text is unchanged. [ADR-SC-005 D-D "Defaults stated by the user and adopted"; Consequences "Incremental re-learn"]

4. **AC-4 — Drift during the prefix (architect conditions 1–4).** If prefix replay drifts at step j < k: (1) only target/observation drift (`TARGET_DRIFT`, locator ambiguity, stale generation) switches the session to learning from j; an assertion `FAIL` or `POLICY_REFUSED` in the prefix **stops the run** (the author did not ask to re-interpret a failing check or a refused action); (2) the switch point j is recorded in the receipt and the published revision marks steps j…k-1 as re-learned, not reused; (3) prefix actions already dispatched are never repeated (at-most-once stands); (4) steps j…k-1 charge the same session model budget. The user-facing statement "steps j… were re-learned because the page changed" is carried in result3 for OBS-008. [ADR-SC-005 D-D "Architect conditions"]

5. **AC-5 — Publication merge.** Finalization builds the new revision = reused prefix snapshots 1…k-1 (or 1…j-1 after drift) + newly learned steps; prefix admissions come from the replay receipts, suffix admissions from the new session; `admissionProvenanceHash` is computed over both; the pure validator (`validator/observedReplay.ts` + `ObservedRevisionVerifier`) verifies the merged plan exactly as a fully learned one; the reused prefix's per-step hashes are byte-identical to the base revision's; `reusedFrom` points at the base revision and step; no hidden replay, no model call during finalize (OBS-006 invariants). Replay drift on a `READY` test ("Learn again from step N") starts the **same** mixed-mode mechanism with k = N. [ADR-SC-005 placement map row OBS-015 "publication merge"; Consequences "drift stops at step N, and 'Learn again' starts a mixed-mode session"; OBS-006 AC-1…AC-5]

6. **AC-6 — Oracles (ADR-SC-005 Verification D-D).** Joined test on the real stack: publish a 10-step plan; edit step 4 ⇒ `GET /tests/{id}` = `NEEDS_RELEARN{firstChangedStepId: step-4}`; the Run's session shows `REPLAYING_PREFIX` then learning; gateway counters are zero during steps 1…3 and nonzero from step 4; the new revision's per-step hashes for 1…3 equal the base revision's and 4…10 differ; a second unchanged Run is a plain replay with zero model calls. Additional cases: insert a step at 4, delete step 4, reorder 4↔5 (each ⇒ k = 4); configuration change ⇒ k = 1; prefix drift at j = 2 ⇒ receipt marks 2…3 re-learned and `relearnedFrom` = 2; assertion FAIL in the prefix ⇒ run stops, nothing re-learned; client `firstLearnStepId` positioned after the server's k ⇒ `409 REVISION_CONFLICT`, `messageCode` `FIRST_LEARN_STEP_NARROWED`; a `secretRef` fill in the reused prefix ⇒ OBS-007 oracles pass unchanged; video from OBS-013 covers both prefix and suffix with offsets. The two incremental corpus cases ("edit step 4 of 10"; "insert a step") are written as repository artifacts for OBS-009's D-C benchmark. [ADR-SC-005 Verification D-D; D-C "The corpus includes at least two incremental cases"]

### W11 writer window (2026-10-17, Dex): the hard-expiry stop-reason race

[V] = I ran it or read it and saw the result. [I] = inference. This is not a QA verdict and does not set Done. All commands ran from `studio-runner/` on Node v24.20.0 (fnm) with `node_modules/.bin`. **Root cause.** [V] Predates W9 and W10. When a session's lease and its hard expiry are the same instant (always, because the backend caps the lease at the expiry), the stop reason was chosen by re-reading `Date.now()` inside the timer callback and comparing it with the expiry (`runtime.ts` and `ownership.ts`, `Date.now() >= expiresAt ? 'EXPIRED' : 'LEASE_LOST'`). A Node timer can fire a fraction of a millisecond before `Date.now()` reaches its due time, so the label was a coin flip at the tie, about 2 % (6/330 = 1.8 % on hard-expiry-only runs, 9/380 = 2.4 % including whole-file runs; the same bytes at HEAD and in final-gates04, -05 and -06). A second path to the same wrong label: a renewal that starts in the last 100 ms, or whose claim budget is positive but shorter than the claim round trip, is aborted at `E - 100 ms` and stopped the session `LEASE_LOST`. Full evidence: `docs/qa/evidence/STUDIO-OBS-015/execution-20261015/w11-hard-expiry01/diagnosis.md`.

**What was applied.** (1) `fix.patch` as written, including the `guided-secret-ownership.test.ts` hunk (decision from the coordinator: kept; its `E - 10 ms` bound on the stop instant is stricter about premature stops than the old poll-phase `Date.now()` read, and the reason is the 5/30 failures with the product already fixed that the old assertion showed under forced early timers). (2) Aria's required change **C-1** (`docs/qa/evidence/STUDIO-OBS-015/execution-20261015/aria-w11-stop-reason-ruling01/ruling.md`).

| Deviation / note | What and why |
|---|---|
| C-1 (Aria, required) | When the lease already equals the session expiry and the renewal claim's budget was set by the 100 ms margin rather than by the 1 s / 10 s cap, a local abort of that claim no longer stops the session; the expiry timers, armed for the same instant, end it as `EXPIRED` at E. Backend refusals (`REVOKED`, `CLAIM_DENIED`, grant-check failures), a cap-bound timeout, and a lease that ends before E keep their reason. The predicate is fixed at claim start from the instants, not from a latency constant. |
