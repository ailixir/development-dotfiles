Studio · How it learns

For every step, Studio looks, decides, tries and proves

  1. 1 · Write a stepThe author describes it in plain English.
  2. 2 · Look at the pageStudio reads what is really on the screen.
  3. 3 · Pick the actionThe AI model picks what to do for this step.
  4. 4 · Try itRuns in a real browser, then checks what changed. If it fails or is unclear, it tries again with what the page showed, or asks the author.
  5. 5 · Prove and keepReplayed without the model. If it passes, the step is learned. Then on to the next step.
The authorStudio

Why it matters. A step is only kept once it has passed on its own. Later runs replay it without the AI model.
